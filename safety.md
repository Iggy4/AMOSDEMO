# Synapse V1 Safety Notices

This project is a demo interface and does not replace certified CNC safety procedures.

- Wear eye protection and appropriate PPE.
- Verify emergency stop location before operation.
- Keep hands clear of spindle, tools, tool changer, and moving axes.
- Confirm machine guards and doors are active.
- Verify coolant flow before cycle start.
- Secure fixtures and workholding before running.
- Do not bypass machine guarding or lockout procedures.
- Follow shop-approved lockout/tagout procedures before maintenance.
