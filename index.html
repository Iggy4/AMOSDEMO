SAFETY NOTICE

Before operating Synapse V1

This demo represents a CNC machine-side monitoring system. Always follow shop safety rules before using real equipment.

  • Wear eye protection and proper PPE.
  • Verify emergency stop location before machine operation.
  • Keep hands clear of spindle, tool changer, and moving axes.
  • Confirm guards, coolant, fixtures, and tooling before cycle start.
  • Never bypass lockout, guarding, or machine safety procedures.
